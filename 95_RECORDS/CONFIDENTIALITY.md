# Information classification

This is a public source repository. Public information includes proposed general corporate structure, brand, engineering contracts and test definitions.

**Never upload:** negotiation-specific partner details, valuations/terms, exact commercial cost assumptions, protected investor material, banking credentials, KYC records, customer personal data, seed phrases or unannounced diligence assessments.

All such records belong in a deliberately permissioned private deal room. The fact of a decision or an unselected jurisdiction may be stated publicly, without exposing the negotiation evidence.
