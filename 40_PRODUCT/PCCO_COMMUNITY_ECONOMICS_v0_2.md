# PCCO v0.2 — Community Participation Economics

**21 November 2026 | Proposed program design — not an issued token, investment solicitation, fixed currency peg or securities determination.**

## Distinct roles
- **PPKT**: named PHP-referenced, reserve-backed payment token. No free emission.
- **PCCO**: meaningfully earned recognition/participation unit tied to verified community impact and access; never a peg, backing asset, substitute redemption reserve or equity claim.
- **KDef**: separately governed, funded development/grant projects; it does not hold PPKT reserve funds or borrow from them for awards.

PCCO's *pico/piso* sound suggests small-scale participation. It NEVER states 1 PCCO equals 1 peso or a peso fraction.

## Alternatives compared
1. **Funded, nontransferable participation credits — RECOMMENDED for P0–P1.** Account-based ledger, earned by verified actions. No speculation, transfer market, staking or resale. Legal review still required before redeemable benefits.
2. Transferable capped community token: improves portability, but opens secondary-market, money transmission/securities/market-conduct risks and introduces governance capture and price volatility. **Not for initial phase**.
3. Profit share / security token: could attract capital, but creates actual financial rights, corporate valuation, securities disclosures and conflicts with inclusive participation; **use separate equity financing**, not PCCO-by-default.

## Initial program definition
**PCCO credit = one noncash participation unit**, NOT fiat, financial asset, payment instrument, deposit, investment return, transferable property or guaranteed benefit. Legal classification and consumer obligations are reviewed if actual benefits are introduced. Trial credits are explicitly synthetic and unrewarded.

Earn through demonstrated and independently approved activity, e.g. completing financial-literacy learning + assessment, helping verified local merchants with approved educational onboarding, volunteering in community programs, documenting local improvements and providing approved nonfinancial peer support. Hard individual/event caps plus manual review protect against Sybil/repeat farming. **No payment for recruitment, fake transaction volume, holding PPKT, staking, pumping a token or linking accounts.**

Use:
- access to education/resources and community-program opportunities;
- advisory voting on community program priorities with **verified personhood/eligibility, capped participation and no per-wallet plutocracy**;
- budgeted partner vouchers or service recognition only if legally authorized and actual fiscal obligations are prefunded and disclosed.

All legal powers of company board, token issuer and KDef remain with their proper governing instruments. Votes cannot transfer safeguarded reserve funds or interfere with redemption rights.

## Reward funding mechanics — separate from PPKT
PCCO benefit programs are funded exclusively by **(a) restricted donors/program grants** and/or **(b) an explicit corporate appropriation from realized, legally distributable operating surplus**, after taxes, liabilities and required capital/risk buffers. Proposed voluntary ceiling for corporate awards: **up to 10% of positive realized distributable operating surplus**, subject to board approval; **if surplus <= 0, corporate-funded awards = 0**. This is a proposed internal budget policy, not an investor profit entitlement, recurring automatic tax, or fixed transfer fee.

At authorization, a reward redemption catalog must recognize worst-case vendor payout obligations, inventory costs or partner subsidies. Never issue more legally enforceable vouchers than the approved prefunded budget can settle. If no binding redemption benefit exists, a PCCO credit has no stated financial exchange price. Do not assume unused credits can fund reserve coverage.

Example (not forecast): distributable surplus ₱100,000 → maximum proposed corporate award appropriation ₱10,000. If each approved benefit has up to ₱2 fulfillment cost, no more than 5,000 such funded benefits may be committed. Awards become liabilities when program terms create enforceable obligations.

## Five-year optional capped token, NOT adopted now
Legacy documents floated 100,000,000 PCCO units over five years. Retain **100M as a planning ceiling only** for possible future legally reviewed Polygon tokenization (not minted or approved today); all unearned allocations remain unissued, with no market maker or sale implied.

Possible *allocation ceilings* (subject to legal/community ratification):
- 60% verified member education/community contribution (60M).
- 20% verified merchant inclusion and partner support (20M).
- 10% regionally verified community projects (10M).
- 10% risk/reconciliation/admin contingency for program integrity (10M).
- 0% reserved for founder/investor purchases or speculative market stabilization.
An illustrative release ceiling of 20M per year for five years is NOT an emission target; balances must be **earned, reviewed, and funded where redemption rights exist**. Over time, a ledger/attestation record may be anchored on Polygon without creating a transferable market.

The token's eventual supply, divisibility, pause/recovery permissions, rights, expirations and data privacy require separate votes and legal advice. A transferable PCCO, secondary trading, yields, ICO, investor benefits or token-price rights requires a new stage and cannot be inferred from the planning ceiling.

## Program safeguards
- no PPKT purchase necessary; no pay-to-participate;
- prohibit exchanges between PCCO and PPKT from project treasury;
- no pledge/lending/collateral use of PCCO for PPKT;
- no issuance based on token trading demand or fiat price oracle;
- distinct ledger, program budget and funding source with dispute/appeal pathway;
- transparency: earned, granted, redeemed, expired, revoked, and budget/commitments; independent impact review;
- prevent repeat awards and automated referral loops;
- disclose any potential data collection, anti-sybil method, and eligibility/appeals clearly;
- keep all investor/equity rights in separate lawful instruments, not community credits.

**Decision:** recommend nontransferable PCCO participation ledger now, optional capped tokenization much later, and program finance isolated from token reserves. No real token issue before governance/regulatory signoff.
