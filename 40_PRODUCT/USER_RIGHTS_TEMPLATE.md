# Customer rights contract template | not in force

This is **not** a user agreement. Before a real service launches, explicitly name: product owner, licensed provider, legal obligor, transaction jurisdiction, supported currency, custody, fee schedule, settlement/availability conditions, reversibility and refund process, dispute resolution, identity/privacy treatment, support contacts, account recovery, limits, changes and termination. Where issued tokens are proposed, add issuer, redemption asset/timing, backing, redemption restrictions, insolvency treatment, reserve proof and public disclosures.

Until names and terms are lawfully approved, UX must say **"Simulation — no money moved, no token issued."**
