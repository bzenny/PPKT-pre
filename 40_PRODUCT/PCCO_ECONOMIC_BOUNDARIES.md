# PCCO — Separate Economic Instrument Decision (working note)

**PPKT is the name of the payment token; PCCO is a sibling concept, not its stabilizer.**

The original 100,000,000 PCCO / five-year release and governance-by-wallet voting are **not adopted**. One-person-one-vote governance is vulnerable to Sybil attack if wallet count is used as identity.

**P0–P1:** off-chain opt-in participation / rewards records; member identity, earning rules, funding and expiry; no sale, transfer, yields, market liquidity or staking; never required for PPKT services.

**P2 candidate:** budget-backed loyalty unit (non-speculative). If cash redemption is added, recognize associated liability and maintain real backing.

**P3 separately governed option:** an actual transferable governance/investment instrument, only after legal classification, investor rights, allocation, vesting, reserve and exit rules are approved. Neither KDef nor PPKT backing pays its yield.

**Language rule:** 'pico/piso' is a branding association, **not an exchange value, peso subdivision, stable peg or guaranteed redemption**.
