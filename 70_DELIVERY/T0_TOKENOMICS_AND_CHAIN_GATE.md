**T0 is proposed and pending human approval. It is NOT a financial launch authorization.**

- [ ] Licensed and liable issuer and verifiable user rights  
- [ ] Independent qualified reserve and legal-custody design, reconciliation and liquidity stress

**No chain-specific production contract or onchain launch may begin merely because T0a has a preferred model.**
