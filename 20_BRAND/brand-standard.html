1. North star and core proposition

From province to city. Connected by opportunity.

The brand joins Filipino provincial heritage, everyday value, and contemporary networks. Its metaphor is a two-way bridge: province and city contribute to one another rather than one "advancing" the other. Position the offering as human-centered financial access infrastructure. Do not promise live remittance, custody, issuance, convertibility or yields ahead of approved evidence.

Brand principles

  1. Rooted — recognizable Filipino place, culture, resilience and dignity without resorting to clichés.
  2. Connected — visible, comprehensible routes and networks between people, households, businesses and communities.
  3. Accountable — legible pricing, status, safeguards, support and honest limitations; polished but never speculative.
  4. Accessible — usable at small sizes, on slow connections, by first-time digital-finance users, in English and Filipino.

3. Symbol 05: Community Heritage Mark

Protected structure: an unmistakable P-shaped main silhouette; woven banig geometry in the lower stem; a sweeping path/bridge crossing from rural forms to city forms; a modest network-node arc toward the upper end. Symbolic detail can be removed at small sizes; meaning must persist.

Interpretation: The P stands for Probinsyano, people and place. The weave expresses continuity of community; fields, tree/house and mountain express provincial roots; bridge and skyline express exchange and connection; nodes reflect modern digital infrastructure; the flowing gold ribbon suggests opportunity, not guaranteed profits.

Master assets: The source files in assets/reference/ are raster concept art. They do not constitute scalable approved vector masters. Commission and approve clean primary and responsive logo drawings (SVG/PDF/PNG) before publication at scale.

4. Responsive logo family and size rules

Tier Typical use Rules Readiness
A · Full-color narrative Pitch deck covers, large hero graphics, signage Detailed heritage scene permitted when symbol is >=160 px wide and remains distinguishable Selected raster reference
B · Simplified primary App icons, web navigation, medium signage Retain bold P silhouette, path/bridge and limited heritage cue; reduce nodes and texture Concept reference, vector redraw required
C · One-color Documents, monochrome print, embroidery Solid navy/white silhouette with clear negative space; no gradients, hairlines or shading Concept reference, vector redraw required
D · Micro-mark 16–32 px favicon, tiny UI status Use a specially redrawn P + path/weave motif, test at each exact rendered size; do not scale down the illustrative master Not yet approved

Proposed limits pending visual testing: detailed symbol >=160 px, simplified >=64 px; 24–48 px one-color icon only after pixel-testing; 16 px must use a dedicated micro-mark. These are proposed thresholds, not verified final production minima.

Clear space: at least the width of the P's lower vertical stem on all sides, measured in the vector master. Do not distort, rotate, stretch, arbitrarily recolor, outline, add glows or place on visually busy backgrounds without protective contrast.

Mandatory QA: color and one-color previews at 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128 and 256 px; white and navy backgrounds; light/dark UI; print proof; horizontal wordmark; screen-reader labels.

5. Color tokens

Token HEX Intended usage
color.navy #0B1F4B Core identity, navigation, headings, trust
color.teal #0F766E Connection, interactive accents, status (never status alone)
color.gold #D4A72C Heritage, highlight, decorative details
color.cream #F8F4E6 Warm document and presentation background
color.ink #1C2938 Main body text
color.white #FFFFFF Clean background, inverse marks

Contrast guidance (WCAG relative luminance): navy on cream ~14.54:1; navy on white ~16.01:1; teal on cream ~4.97:1; white on teal ~5.47:1. Gold on cream ~2.04:1 fails normal text contrast and must be decorative only. Use navy text on gold instead (~7.14:1). These numeric combinations do not replace per-component UI contrast and focus-state testing.

Distribution principle: navy and cream carry the page; teal handles interaction; gold provides selective warmth. Avoid gold-on-cream body copy and gradients in the one-color icon.

6. Typography, iconography and layout

Suggested display face: Montserrat Bold/SemiBold.
Suggested UI/body face: Inter or Source Sans 3; fallback to system sans-serif.
Typography must support Filipino text and the peso sign, use real local font licensing/hosting checks, and avoid excessive letter spacing in long paragraphs. Minimum standard UI body: 16 CSS px; labels ideally >=14 CSS px, but all interfaces require actual WCAG review.

Layout: mobile-first card structures, clear top-level actions, ample breathing room, repeatable 8 px rhythm, visible progress and help paths; use photos that portray people with dignity, not pity. The weave is a subtle edge motif rather than a dense background under text.

Supporting icons: one coherent line-icon set with consistent stroke, viewport and corner radius. Themes: send/receive, family, business, support, community, network, verified status, ledger receipt; no visual claims of regulatory clearance or insured deposits.

7. Brand voice, language and claims

Voice: helpful, grounded, approachable and precise. Explain concepts before acronyms. Avoid hype, trading culture, guaranteed returns and assumptions that rural users are inexperienced.

Preferred: “Know where your transfer stands.” / “Clear information. Connected communities.” / “See the fee before you confirm.”
Filipino working copy: “Mula probinsya hanggang lungsod, konektado.” Language/localization review required before public use.

Avoid until proven and authorized: “1 PPKT = 1 PHP, guaranteed”; “regulated by the BSP”; “available in GCash and Maya”; “risk-free”; “insured”; “earn daily rewards”; “official peso token”; “your funds are always safe.” Any illustration involving balances, payments, redemption, coins, exchange listings, or partner logos must carry a prominent Concept preview — not a live financial service label.

8. Media and campaign system

Hero: parallel province/city scenes connected by a real or abstract bridge; clear human relationship and both-way network movement. Scenic motifs should rotate beyond a single stereotyped region; no real partner logos without authorization.

Templates: website hero (16:9); social landscape (1.91:1); social square (1:1); vertical story (9:16); pitch cover (16:9); app store icon (1:1); email header (wide). Create export presets in a design tool after final logo vectors exist. Keep clear space around the mark and place CTA copy in high-contrast solid panels.

Media sequence: Awareness (mission) → Understanding (how it would work) → Trust (evidence and limitations) → Participation (permitted pilot actions) → Launch (only after gates). Never use mock screens to imply actual cash movement.

9. PCCO and KDef identity architecture

PCCO: an endorsed sibling identity, not PPKT's replacement. Carry navy, teal, cream, and the weave/path network DNA; differentiate with a circular community/seed motif and warmer gold emphasis. Its “pico/piso” origin informs sound and concept, not an agreed unit conversion, fixed price, yield or member rights. All such claims await the Charter v0.2 product decision and legal review.

KDef: operational stewardship label; use the parent type and navy/teal, with documentation-first identity. Do not draw a tradeable coin logo for KDef or suggest development capital is funded from protected customer backing.

10. Approval, governance and ICM/MasterSys CORE mapping

The brand direction is creatively selected but not equivalent to authorization of token issuance, corporate registration, rights, or public launch. Keep decisions and proofs traceable. The Charter v0.2 must define corporate/legal identity, product rights, authorized counterparties, PCCO/KDef mandates, financial boundaries, board and user representation, and evidence-based permissions before development of live financial workflows.

ICM = Interpretable Context Methodology. MasterSys simplified CORE: protect local, inspectable files as the durable source of truth, with selective context loading. Structure appears when responsibility appears. Priority: FOLDERS → HTML → Markdown → JSON → YAML. Use 00_PRIME/PRIME.html for explicitly draft human authority; MASTER.html for bounded project orientation; root and scoped CONTEXT.md for limited instruction; 20_BRAND/brand-standard.html as canonical human-readable brand knowledge, 20_BRAND/brand-guide.md as Markdown export; 20_BRAND/design-tokens.json as structured tokens. 10_ARCHITECTURE/rules.json and registry.json govern technical declarations when adopted. Avoid premature agent networks, multi-repo complexity or unnecessary microservices.
