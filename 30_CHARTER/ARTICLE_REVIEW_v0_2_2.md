## Structural issues corrected in candidate

1. The earlier HTML charter still contained the Philippine-only OperatingCo model and contradicted Markdown Article 2. Regenerate HTML from the same ratification candidate Markdown.
2. The full package had more files than the initial live PR. Reconcile canonical files and hashes before calling GitHub synchronized; distinguish reference raster art from final vector assets.
3. Provisional user-council and KDef decision rights cannot be described as presently exercisable. Interim protection is a **development policy**, not an invented legal body.
4. Parent-board authority for simulation must not be conflated with any future international legal entity: use appointed project sponsors for development approvals only.
5. Confidential detailed operating-cost assumptions must not appear in public docs. Compare the parent-country options using audited full-group cost and safeguards rather than claims of automatic offshore safety.
6. A policy/automated check can never self-certify legal counsel, registered entities, actual customer backing, banking solvency, investment funding, or independent statutory power.
7. G0 and G1 must not block the ability to perform harmless synthetic engineering until a corporation has been constituted. A separate D0→D1 development-only authorization is required.

## Proposed ratification disposition

- **Accept as development policy defaults:** mission, identity, PH-first delivery, CORE-first software, no live assets or issued tokens, synthetic M1.1, customer protection, confidentiality, signoff and evidence discipline.
- **Reserve until external evidence:** parent legal seat, directors, beneficial owners, actual bank/custodian and partners, investor cap table, tax, funding and permits.
- **Ratification eligibility:** founders/competent proponents may approve *development-only* policy with names and signatures; corporate/financial legal effect requires proper entity formation, bylaws, counsel and subsequent distinct resolutions.
