# Group and operating architecture — UNSELECTED, NOT INCORPORATED

The preferred design preserves **two parent domicile options**: Singapore, or Dubai with the exact legal jurisdiction identified. Neither choice is ratified. The Philippines is the preferred lower-cost technology, support and operational-delivery base and the primary intended customer market; it need not be the parent location.

```text
ParentCo (Singapore OR selected Dubai regime; TBD)
  ├─ ProductOps: technology/IP/customer interface (legal form TBD)
  ├─ PHServiceCo or authorized PH-facing partners (route TBD)
  └─ KDef: restricted development stewardship (legal form TBD)

PPKT issuer: not authorized / not in the group until separate decision
PCCO: unissued / rights undecided
```

The draft proposes a five-seat parent board, independent five-seat user council, and seven-member KDef committee with 5/7 proposed disbursement authorization. All offices, entity types, individual appointments, investor reserved matters, shareholder percentages, funding, regulatory authority and custody are unverified.

A parent abroad **cannot replace Philippine registrations**, nor may customer funds be moved from customer reserves into an offshore corporate treasury. Actual transfer and custody structure must follow executed partner terms, segregation law, AML and tax review.

### Why domicile remains open

Prospective partners have indicated interest in capital and management location, but no commitment or final location has been made. Confidential identities, proposals and economic terms are not recorded publicly. Jurisdiction selection requires a scored due-diligence memorandum and professional legal advice, not a subjective claim that any country is intrinsically safer.

### Operational footprint versus financial control

A foreign parent is a candidate for fundraising, board governance, IP ownership and treasury oversight, while Philippine employees and contractors may provide development, support and lawful day-to-day operations. Customer money, regulated permissions and legally liable counterparties must be separately mapped for each service. The project must not imply that all funds will be held offshore or that offshore incorporation avoids Philippine law.

**Financial-location decision:** use a side-by-side model for total consolidated costs and independent bank/custody/legal risk; an internally cited PH delivery cost advantage is not sufficient evidence of the consolidated cost of an international-parent/PH-delivery group. The quantified range stays in confidential diligence until validated and cleared for disclosure.
