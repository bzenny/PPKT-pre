# Charter and authority — v0.2 draft

Primary: [Building Charter v0.2](PPKT_Building_Charter_v0_2.md) (also [HTML](PPKT_Building_Charter_v0_2.html)).

`authority-matrix.json`: proposed approval graph. `corporate-structure.md`: proposed entity/counterparty diagram. `product-boundaries.md`: PPKT/PCCO/KDef rights and exclusions. `adoption-checklist.md`: signed evidence required before ratification. `LEGAL_REVIEW.md`: research watchlist. `95_RECORDS/decisions.json` is the decision ledger, not the record of legal rights.

**There is no adoption resolution, selected director, bank/custodian, licensed partner, legally binding holder term, issued token or protected account.** No status is silently promoted by a GitHub merge. Human sign-off, legal review, real authorizations and independent evidence must precede any regulated activity.
