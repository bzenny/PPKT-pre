# Ratification checklist | all items require human evidence

- [ ] Legal name reserved; SEC corporate registration/authority or documented alternative.
- [ ] Final articles/bylaws, beneficial ownership and governance structure examined by Philippine counsel.
- [ ] Identified lawfully appointed directors/officers and signatory matrix.
- [ ] Final service user segment and actual product permissions adopted.
- [ ] Valid capital, operating budget, exposure limits and KDef allocation/funding plan.
- [ ] Customer/partner obligations and custody/issuer classifications reviewed in writing.
- [ ] PPKT issuance **explicitly rejected/deferred or separately legally approved**.
- [ ] PCCO instrument **explicitly deferred or separately legally reviewed**.
- [ ] KDef seats, conflicts, 5/7 logic, bank/signing mapping and fallback specified.
- [ ] Independent user council role and enforceable boundaries established, or marked deferred without false claims.
- [ ] Privacy officer, security officer, complaint channel and incident owners designated.
- [ ] Charter signed, adopted version/effective date recorded, exclusions individually reviewed.
- [ ] Independent evidence / scope / approvals stored privately and referenced by hash or URL where permitted.
