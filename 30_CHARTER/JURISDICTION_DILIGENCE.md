| Operational resilience | Test independent custody, backup and reconciliation | Same test | Independent attestation and exit tests |
| PH delivery cost advantage | Retain PH product, support and lawful operations where practical | Same PH delivery baseline | Separately cost CAPEX/OPEX, foreign-parent overhead, taxes, FX and assurance |
| PH financial counterparty exposure | Identify lawful accounts and independent safeguards | Same, regardless of parent location | Solvency/counterparty review, concentration limits, contingency liquidity |

**Decision rubric:** functional legal route (must pass), demonstrable bank/custody safeguards (must pass), legitimate investor governance, real local substance, consumer rights, privacy, operations and total cost. No weighted score can override a failed legal or safety gate.

## Public confidentiality rule

Only record the existence of a *jurisdiction decision*, not identity or terms of parties in live negotiations. Detailed investor and jurisdiction due diligence belongs in an access-controlled offline or private deal room.

**Cost discipline:** maintain the quoted substantial but unverified PH delivery advantage only in private diligence as an unverified planning assumption, not as audited net group savings. It must be reconciled against an explicit comparator, cost basket, time horizon and evidence. Do not publicly disclose negotiated rates or partner terms.

**Safety discipline:** a jurisdiction is not rated safe based solely on its name or investor preference. Apply mandatory legal, reserve/custody and operational recovery gates first; only compare eligible options on cost and investment feasibility afterward.
