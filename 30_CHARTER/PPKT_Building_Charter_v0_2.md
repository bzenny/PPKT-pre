# PPKT Building Charter v0.2.2 — International governance / Philippine delivery draft

**PH Probinsyano Token — Corporate, Product, Governance and Technical Building Charter**  
**Version:** 0.2.2-DRAFT | **Prepared:** 20 November 2026 | **Status:** PROPOSED — NOT RATIFIED  
**Supersedes:** nothing until formal adoption; develops v0.1 (7 October 2026).  
**Jurisdictional design assumption:** Philippines-first service market and preferred delivery base; international parent management location (Singapore or specified Dubai regime) remains unselected.

> **Authority warning:** This document is a design and adoption proposal. It is not proof of incorporation, authority to issue currency/tokens, a regulatory permit, bank relationship, existing board, protected trust account, or public fundraising authorization. It neither conveys consumer rights nor changes historical obligations until the appropriate legal instruments and counterparties are in force.

## Preamble — Preserve the purpose, correct the mechanism

The mission is to make practical financial access understandable, dependable and affordable to families and underserved communities, especially across the province-to-city relationship. The project prioritizes fairness, human dignity, inclusion, education, accountable stewardship and **Gradual Growth Progression (GGp)**. Sustainable operations are necessary, but neither speculative returns nor recruitment is a condition of basic participation. PPKT's historic multi-instrument plans are source material, not binding requirements.

**Operating commitment:** Build a real useful service, prove an auditable obligation-to-settlement trail, protect user interests, and add blockchain only for measurable benefit. Market demand does not authorize financial activity. No holder is promised interest, a price floor, appreciation or guaranteed redemption before a lawful issuer assumes a defined obligation.

## Article 1 — Name, identity and status

1. Public ecosystem brand: **PH Probinsyano Token**. Market/project identifier: **PPKT**. Selected logo direction: **Symbol 05 — Community Heritage Mark**, with compact and monochrome variants subject to vectorization and legibility testing. **PPKP** remains a legacy historical shorthand; do not use it as a new market identifier.
2. **PCCO** is a *provisional* sibling identity originally associated with “Pico/Piso.” The phrase conveys accessible, small-scale participation, **not** a claim that PCCO equals one peso or a peso subdivision, is legal tender, earns income, or is issued.
3. **KDef** names a proposed independently governed development-fund function. It does not imply that a fund has been endowed, a charity incorporated, or assets safeguarded.
4. All public-facing financial, financial-promotion and development statements must visibly distinguish `concept`, `simulation`, `partner-operated live service`, `issued asset`, and `licensed activity`. In v0.2's present unratified state, everything remains **concept or sandbox**.

## Article 2 — International parent options, Philippine delivery and lawful authority

**Status: proposed; no company, investor commitment, domicile or bank relationship has been confirmed.** The management and parent company may be constituted in Singapore or a legally specified Dubai regime, subject to investor and legal diligence. **The Philippines remains the preferred location for technology development, support and lawful service delivery because of its expected cost advantage.** An overseas parent choice does not imply an overseas workforce or transfer of all customer money offshore.

- **ParentCo:** optional future entity responsible for investment governance, board strategy, legal IP ownership and company treasury oversight within its actual licensed and tax-authorized scope. No preferred jurisdiction has been adopted.
- **PH Delivery / ProductOps:** Philippine-based engineering, support, accounting operations and permitted administration; entity, subcontracting, labor and privacy terms require lawful formation or contracts.
- **PH-facing customer service:** a separately authorized PH entity or relevant licensed partners, depending on service and obligations. Offshore incorporation cannot bypass Philippine requirements.
- **Future PPKT issuer:** no issuer designated or authorized. Any instrument needs separately approved issuance, reserve, redemption, custody, solvency and territorial legal analysis.
- **KDef:** development stewardship funded only from identified unencumbered resources, not customer reserves.
- **PCCO:** neither issued nor automatically an investment, governance right or stablecoin backing.

**Separate location decisions:** location of workers, management, bank/custody, treasury, settlement, legal customer liability, regulated service provider and financial statements are separate matters. No proposal or investor discussion grants authority over a category not explicitly defined.

**Cost model:** initial proposals indicate material lower costs for PH-based delivery, but quantified percentages, partner negotiations and commercial terms must remain confidential until independently verified and cleared for disclosure. The consolidated group budget must include foreign-parent substance, compliance, audit, banking, cross-border tax, FX and contingencies; a domestic labor cost advantage is not automatically the same as total international-group savings.

**Risk model:** concerns about internal Philippine banking, institution solvency, oversight continuity and financial instability are material issues for diligence. Do not state categorically that all PH institutions are unsafe, or that offshore registration solves those risks. Verify counterparty strength, real fund segregation, liquidity, concentration limits, statements and independent attestations, continuity and legal enforceability in each relevant market.

**Capital negotiations:** Singapore and Dubai partners are prospective only; no terms, valuation, investment, owner, board control, bank account or fundraising permission is established. Protect partner identities, discussions, proposed allocations and detailed cost data in an access-controlled private deal room, not in public GitHub.

**No phantom entities:** This charter is not evidence that an international parent, Philippine subsidiary, issuer or trustee exists, or that any investment or token offering is permitted.

## Article 3 — Four floors and modular boundaries

- **Floor 1 / public:** onboarding, education, user journeys, quotes and fees, receipts, customer-support cases, community participation and accessible recovery. Public activity never makes customer identity public.
- **Floor 2 / operations:** provider adapters, double-entry accounting, settlement workflows, reconciliation, operational risk, records, support and incident handling.
- **Floor 3 / governance:** board-level oversight, budget and capital policies, conflicts, product authority, risk limits, KDef mandate, controls and protected amendments. A board approves policy, **not** every ordinary payment.
- **Basement / research:** discovery, scenario modeling, synthetic simulations, prototypes, testing and release rehearsal. It has no live customer funds or production signing keys by default.

A floor means **responsibility**, not technical privilege or microservice. Every module is least-privileged; reuse of shared controls is mandatory where applicable.

## Article 4 — Corporate governance and user independence

**Target OperatingCo governance model:** five directors, with a target of at least two independent non-executive perspectives, as a proposed internal design **not a statement of statutory requirement**. Actual director number, nationality, eligibility, elections, fiduciary duties, appointment of officers and amendment mechanics must comply with corporate law and filed documents. Initial authorized roles are `Board`, `CEO/Service Owner`, `Finance and Reconciliation Lead`, `Security and Privacy Lead`, `Legal/Compliance Lead` and `Independent Reviewer`; names remain vacant until recorded.

- **Corporate board** exercises legally assigned corporate powers; no user council or token-holder vote purports to displace mandatory statutory duties.
- **User Council (proposed five seats):** a distinct, non-investor representation body with independent membership verification, transparent selection/terms, complaints and protected-policy review. A seat cannot be bought with holdings, split across wallets, or assigned to a capital-weighted proxy. Council powers need enforceable corporate bylaws/contractual implementation before being claimed publicly.
- **KDef Committee (proposed seven seats):** independent stewardship under Article 9; no current appointments implied. Separate the committee's project-selection and disbursement authority from the ledger operator and financial controller.
- **Oversight:** directors and committee members disclose financial interests, recuse where necessary, and record conflict decisions. A conflict is not cured by silent abstention.
- **Separation of duties:** one actor cannot propose, approve, execute and independently reconcile the same exceptional money movement. Security incidents require independent after-action review.

## Article 5 — Membership and participation rights

Basic access must not require a crypto-wallet, token purchase, investment, staking, recruitment, or ownership. Users must be told what the product is, who owes them money (if anyone), the full fee before authorization, limits, risks, time to settlement, privacy uses, support channel, complaint/escalation route and practical exit.

For modules using a licensed partner, rights and responsibilities must reflect the **actual contract and allocation of legal liability**, including custody, chargebacks, reversals, failed payouts, lost access and records. Project marketing cannot promise a right that the contract does not provide.

## Article 6 — PPKT product architecture and reserved token decision

**Stage P0 — design/sandbox:** PPKT is a project identifier and simulated unit where appropriate; there is **no publicly issued PPKT token**, traded market, binding peg, exchange rate, or reserve-backed redeemable claim. Sandbox balances and images must be labeled simulated.

**Stage P1 — partner-led useful service (preferred MVP):** OperatingCo offers an access and workflow interface to an authorized provider, within the exact scope legal counsel and the provider approve. Customer money is controlled by the appropriate licensed entity; the record of authority documents who owes the obligation and who handles each dispute. OperatingCo does not automatically become the issuer, banker, custodian, remittance operator or VASP simply by using an API.

**Stage P2 — optional issuance decision:** No mint, sale, list, public distribution or exchange occurs until: (a) legal classification and permissible route are confirmed; (b) issuer is identified and authorized; (c) a written peso claim, issuance/redeem process, custody and asset-ownership treatment are enforceable; (d) qualifying liquid reserves, independent attestations and segregation are operational; (e) an audited threat model and appropriate contract/security audits exist; (f) independent governance and public disclosures approve. A **peso-linked ambition** is not the same as an enforceable 1:1 peso claim. PPKT issuance is **not approved** in v0.2.

**Mechanism constraints:** no algorithmic demand-triggered minting to defend price, no use of unissued PCCO as minimum reserve backing, no borrowing or investment of customer backing to fund operating costs, no undisclosed rehypothecation, no self-certified verification of liabilities and collateral. If future issuance is permitted, every mint follows verified settled backing and authorizations; every burn/redeem preserves remaining unpaid obligations until cash settlement is confirmed and reconciled. Technical on-chain finality alone is not proof of fiat payout.

## Article 7 — PCCO decision boundary

PCCO's historic governance, reward and trading functions are intentionally **de-coupled**. Its **current decision is DEFER**: no transferable coin, investment offer, yield, staking, exchange, airdrop, tokenomics supply, voting-by-wallet, or promise of earned monetary benefit. The brand may be shown only as a clearly identified unissued concept.

Permitted preliminary experiments: non-financial community suggestions; an opt-in, non-transferable reputation/participation score with privacy safeguards; and independently funded service discounts **only after explicit terms and funding authorization**. These are separate ideas, not automatically a crypto token. Any future PCCO instrument requires a standalone decision, legal classification, anti-manipulation review, funding source, privacy review, and rights/disclosures.

## Article 8 — Financial architecture and fund separation

The fund register must distinguish at minimum:

| Fund / ledger partition | Permitted source | Permitted use | Protected boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| `CUSTOMER_OBLIGATIONS` | Valid customer liabilities or partner notifications | Discharge owed customer value | Never fund operations, KDef, PCCO rewards or speculative investments |
| `CUSTOMER_BACKING` (future only) | Settled eligible reserve assets for a lawful issuer | Required backing/redemptions | Custodian and legal separation must be real; no token backing by PCCO |
| `OPERATING_CAPITAL` | Equity, legitimate revenue, permitted contributions | Payroll, infrastructure, lawful operating costs | Does not count as customer reserves |
| `KDEF_CAPITAL` | Explicit, lawful grants/allocations from unencumbered capital | Approved development projects | Cannot divert customer rights/obligations |
| `REWARD_BUDGET` | Explicitly approved and funded budget | Published, capped and lawful participation benefits | No presumed yield; reward liabilities must be recorded |
| `LENDING_CAPITAL` (future only) | Separately authorized risk capital | Legally permitted loan services | Never commingle protected reserves or customer obligations |

Legal custody and bank accounts must reflect the product's actual requirements; merely naming ledger partitions does not legally segregate assets. One asset cannot support incompatible promises. Any movement requires budget authority, named owner, double-entry journal, explicit source/destination and reconciliation trail. No negative balances or hidden supply adjustments.

## Article 9 — KDef authority and independence

KDef is the project-development stewardship program, **not** a blanket investment trust or a source of token collateral. A future seven-member committee, if credible and lawfully established, may authorize ordinary KDef commitments/disbursements only with **5/7 votes**, recorded on the exact beneficiary, amount, scope, conditions, timing and conflicts. Execution requires **5/7 controlled signing** where adopted and technically possible, with two-person controls on preparation and reconciliation. This 5/7 standard cannot bypass mandatory banking/corporate rules. No 4/7 alternate transfer path.

No grant executes based solely on a committee vote: funds must exist in KDef capital, milestones must be evidenced, recipients checked, and executed payment reconciled. KDef officers do not override board fiduciary obligations or customer rights. If a seven-seat independent committee cannot be staffed, **KDef spending beyond ordinary, approved OperatingCo project costs stays on hold** pending a documented alternative.

## Article 10 — Authority and controlled decisions

The authority schedule is operationalized in `30_CHARTER/authority-matrix.json` and must agree with this text. Routine transactions operate under published policies and customer consent; board/executive discretion does not override settlement facts.

- Product/service activation: service owner proposal → finance, security/privacy and legal review → board authorization → operator implementation → independent release evidence.
- Exceptional fund movement: initiator → independent approver → controlled execution → independent reconciliation; changing destination/amount requires reapproval.
- KDef grants: 5/7 mandate and applicable signature threshold, segregated resources and independent reconciliation.
- Emergency pause: assigned incident responder may pause, revoke credentials and quarantine at-risk activities immediately, **not** transfer protected customer funds or rewrite financial obligations. Independent review within 24 hours; extraordinary powers expire or renew explicitly within 72 hours. Restart needs documented containment and reconciliation.
- Protected change: proposed baseline of **30 days' notice** and independent **two-thirds of the full governing body and full user council** with recusal/conflict provisions; definitions of seated members, vacancies, rounding and any required shareholder/statutory approvals remain mandatory adoption details. No vote may extinguish owed customer money or waive law.

## Article 11 — Shared technical controls / MasterSys CORE

**Progressive complexity is law:** start `CORE`, earn `STRUCTURED`, defer `INTELLIGENT`. The order of context and authority is **FOLDERS → HTML → Markdown → JSON → YAML**, not a justification to ignore controlling legal instruments, implemented controls or human ratification.

Core files: `manifest.json`, `MASTER.html`, `CONTEXT.md`, `00_PRIME/PRIME.html`, `10_ARCHITECTURE/rules.json`, `10_ARCHITECTURE/registry.json`, `20_BRAND/brand-standard.html`, `20_BRAND/brand-guide.md`, `20_BRAND/design-tokens.json`, `30_CHARTER/PPKT_Building_Charter_v0_2.md`, and `90_VALIDATION/`. Authority goes from lawful human approvals to registered specification to verified code, **not** from AI-generated records or automated checkmarks to independent permission.

Shared mandatory capabilities when relevant: stable identity and membership; role/resource-purpose authorization; balanced append-only journal entries; versioned approval/workflows; evidence IDs connecting request, ledger, provider and chain; provider adapters and replay protection; controlled signing and secrets; accessible interface/support; monitoring/recovery; release and documentation records.

The initial implementation may be a **modular monolith** with a transactional database, a durable outbox/job mechanism and a simulator. No premature microservices, custom blockchain, independent DAO, automated treasury, bridge, DEX or multi-token mint engine. Developer/AI access shall not have production signing or customer-fund authority.

## Article 12 — Payment and settlement discipline

The first end-to-end user story is a **synthetic family transfer** from a provincial sender to an urban recipient. It must include: readable quote and fees; consent; eligibility/limits; unique request ID; reserved amount/obligation; exactly-one logical provider instruction; realistic pending/unknown results; eventual provider proof; independent reconciliation; receipt; complaint handling; and safe recovery.

If a provider times out, keep `pending_unknown`, query the same external reference and **never blindly resend**. Duplicated/out-of-order callbacks are idempotent. Corrections are linked journal entries, not destructive history edits. Token burn does not extinguish a payable prior to proven fiat settlement. Source of truth is declared per fact: issuer for legal obligation, custodian for reserve asset, payment provider for its settlement record, chain for its on-chain events, and internal ledger for accounted claims.

## Article 13 — Privacy, safety, operational recovery

Use purpose-limited data, strong role-based access, segregated environments, minimal retention, encrypted storage/transport and a documented lawful privacy basis. Do not publish personally identifying or sensitive information on public chains. Simulated and anonymized test fixtures by default; production-derived data requires separately approved handling.

Must-test failures: duplicate submissions, delayed/duplicated/out-of-order callbacks, partial payout, returned funds, unknown status, account takeover, key loss, reserve shortfall (where applicable), ledger mismatch, outage, restore, and reconciliation after rollback. Test chain reorganizations and stale oracle feeds only for modules that actually use them. Restoration is incomplete until liabilities and external effects reconcile.

## Article 14 — Independent product verifier (`PPKT VERIFY`)

The verifier has **two independent responsibilities**: (1) release integrity—governance evidence, schema consistency, ledger invariants, operational controls, authorization and manual attestations; (2) competitive evidence—comparable *measured* total cost, usable settlement time, completion rate, task success and complaint recovery against at least one non-crypto baseline and other relevant providers.

Tests must return `UNKNOWN/BLOCKED` when evidence is missing, stale or unqualified. An automated passing test **cannot** assert a law license, a signed board resolution, bank reserves, third-party attestation, security audit, user adoption, or a production release. Evidence entries include source, scope, date, reviewer and limitations. A human reviewer cannot turn failed test evidence into success merely by approval.

## Article 15 — Development and release lifecycle

`G0` corporate/rights/adoption; `G1` repository and reproducible governance tests; `G2` user validated synthetic prototype; `G3` balanced ledger and strict authorization; `G4` staging API, monitoring and restore rehearsal; `G5` optional chain-testnet evaluation and adapter; `G6` independent verifier and comparable evidence; `G7` **pre-launch** disclosure, legal/partner review, security and operational sign-off. Gates are defined in `70_DELIVERY/release-gates.json`, with separate `technical_ci` and `prelaunch_readiness` checks. Prior steps do not authorize live money.

The first committed milestone is a working synthetic transaction and a demonstrably blocked release if corporate, legal or independence evidence has not been provided. Any new token, partner, payment rail or regulated financial activity re-enters the relevant approval gates. A sandbox demo may be demonstrated with prominent disclosures but cannot masquerade as a live or licensed product.

## Article 16 — Adoption, supremacy and protected revision

To adopt v0.2, the legally competent proponents must specify: (1) entity/formation path and provisional capitalization; (2) founding officers/directors and conflicts; (3) approved first user group and service; (4) funding and authorized expenses; (5) PPKT holder-rights/issuer decision; (6) PCCO decision; (7) KDef formation and selection policy; (8) user representation enforceability; (9) legal approvals and counterparties; (10) accessible operational/legal disclosures; (11) charter authority/signing and amendment schedule; (12) date, signatures and exceptions.

**Until then status remains `PROPOSED`**, even if files are committed to GitHub. No automated agent may mark a charter ratified, issuer authorized, reserve attested or committee appointed. Registered approvals must include source reference, signatories, dates, affected scope, conflicts and effective version. If law or executed contract conflicts with this proposal, halt affected activity and seek counsel; no code comment or GitHub vote overrides governing law.

## Article 17 — Comparative location, capital and financial-risk authority

1. **Choose by evidence, not by assertion.** Evaluate at least three comparable configurations: Philippines-concentrated, Singapore-parent/PH-delivery and Dubai-parent/PH-delivery. Dubai's exact zone and regulator must be known before comparison.
2. **Protect cost advantage.** Philippine delivery is the operating default unless a demonstrated alternative improves risk-adjusted performance. Measure consolidated group startup and recurring costs: PH employment/vendors, international management substance, corporate compliance, treasury, bank/custody, independent audits, FX, data, tax, legal, security and stress-contingency.
3. **Numerical claims must be verified.** Partner-proposal savings are unverified internal estimates and are excluded from public performance representations. Record cost basket, date, comparator, evidence, sensitivity, decision authority and independent reviewer in confidential diligence.
4. **Banking and solvency gate.** Before handling financial assets, verify each relevant bank or custodian, its permitted role and financial condition; ensure appropriate safeguarded fund ownership, enforceable rights, segregation, concentration limits, liquidity reserves, independent account statements and tested recovery plans.
5. **Cross-border control.** Exposures, intercompany movements, capital acceptance and treasury allocation require lawful purpose, independent approval, tax and FX assessment, recorded beneficiary, KYC/AML as applicable, and independent reconciliation. Customer liabilities must never be used as company runway.
6. **Investor conditionality.** Investor preference for parent location is a negotiation input, not proof of legal or financial authorization. No governance rights or capital commitments may be claimed without executed terms, reviewed rights, actual settlement and authorized records.
7. **Regulatory and local consumer rights.** A foreign parent does not remove Philippine customer-market or partner requirements. Each licensed activity and offer jurisdiction must be identified and legally reviewed before funds move or offers are made.
8. **Launch blocks.** No real-money activity until each separate location, counterparties, issuer rights (if any), reserve claims, tax and regulatory requirements, continuity and customer redress controls are evidenced and approved. CI cannot ratify them.
