- [ ] International parent candidate and exact legal zone lawfully selected
- [ ] PH delivery cost estimate verified by a comparable, fully consolidated cost model
- [ ] Staff location separated from corporate board, legal customer liability, reserve custody and settlement
- [ ] Bank/custodian solvency, legal segregation, credit concentration and liquidity independently evaluated

- [ ] Business continuity, incident recovery, FX and provider exit scenario tested

Missing evidence blocks real-money and investment launch; green CI is never proof of those permissions.
