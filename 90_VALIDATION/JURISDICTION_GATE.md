# Mandatory jurisdiction and capital gate

- [ ] Parent jurisdiction and legal zone selected in signed decision (Singapore or exact Dubai regime).
- [ ] Entity formation and actual beneficial ownership verified (if formed).
- [ ] Board/investor rights and protective user rights reconciled with legal duties.
- [ ] Philippines customer-facing activity and local registration/partner licensing determined.
- [ ] No false claim of reserve backing, solvency, stablecoin classification or licence.
- [ ] Bank/custody controls, independent attestations and exposure diversification reviewed.
- [ ] Local delivery-cost estimate validated against a defined comparator and consolidated cost model.
- [ ] Funding/corporate oversight jurisdiction is separated from places of work, customer liability, custody and settlement.
- [ ] PH and international counterparty failure, liquidity, FX and recovery scenarios rehearsed.
- [ ] Investor negotiations and private data excluded from public repository.
- [ ] Financing accepted only after valid agreements, securities-law classification, due diligence and account controls.

**Failure of any mandatory item blocks public financial operations and investor solicitation.**
